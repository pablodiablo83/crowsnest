    How the track is recorded

    An iPhone only reports its position while Crow's Nest is open on the screen, so the track has gaps while the phone is locked or the app is closed. Keep the app open at the nav station to fill them. Legs longer than 3 hours between positions are drawn dashed because the path in between is not known. Distances are straight lines between positions, so they are approximate and slightly short on a winding course.
