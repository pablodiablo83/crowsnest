Crow’s NestWhat it is
App description

One screen for the person in charge of the vessel

Crow’s Nest keeps the records a master is responsible for, hours of rest and the voyage track, alongside the information needed to plan the next passage: tides, wind, the inshore waters forecast and an Atlantic weather chart. Crew tap WORK or REST; the server works out compliance against the rest rules and shows what is within the limit, what is not, and what is unknown because nothing was logged.

Who it is for

  • Masters and skippers of small commercial, charter and sail-training vessels who must keep hours-of-rest records.
  • Crew, each on their own phone, seeing and changing only their own hours.
  • Shore management needing a clean, exportable record and a change history.

How it is built

  • The server is the authority. It checks every record and decides compliance; phones record what happened and send it when they can.
  • Offline first on the phone. Taps, new entries, End voyage and positions are saved on the phone and sent in order, each applied once.
  • Self-hosted. Runs on the owner’s own server behind a secure tunnel; data stays with the operator.
  • Nothing is erased. Edits and deletions are audited and can be undone; the change history names the phone or account.

Getting a phone connected

On the web, the master opens Customise › Phones › Pair a phone, chooses Master or one crew member, and types the one-time code into the app. A lost phone is removed in one tap; its records stay.

Crow’s NestHours of rest
Core feature

Hours of rest, checked continuously

Each crew member taps WORK or REST as they change over. Rolling 24-hour and 7-day windows are checked at every moment of the voyage, not just now, and the record is ready to print or export.

14 hat most between rest periods
  • Three honest verdicts: within the limit, below the limit, or not known. Unlogged time is never silently counted as rest.
  • “You can keep working for…” shows how long until the next limit is reached.
  • Last 7 days as a bar per day: rest, work, not logged; tap a block to change it.
  • Voyages per crew member with a joining declaration (rested at joining, records acknowledged).
  • Manual entries for time logged late, with overlap checks, undo and restore.
  • Crew phones see and change only their own hours; the master sees everyone.
  • Record and export: printable record for endorsement, CSV and full JSON data, change history.

Rules follow MCA/STCW hours of rest; thresholds are configurable for the instrument that applies to the vessel. Crow’s Nest is a record-keeping aid: the master remains responsible for compliance.

Voyage map

The track, recorded as you work

  • Positions on every WORK/REST tap, at End voyage, on “Log position now”, and in the background while a voyage is open (iPhone: while the app is running).

Crow’s NestTides
Passage planning

Tides worked out from real gauge records

Crow’s Nest analyses a year of readings from UK National Tide Gauge Network stations and predicts heights and high and low waters itself, so tides keep working offline once loaded.

  • Now: height above chart datum, rising or falling, next high and low water, springs/neaps.
  • Tidal curve by day, with a slider for the height at any time.
  • Search a place or port; the nearest gauge or your own secondary port is used, and the page says which and how far away.
  • Secondary ports from your almanac differences (Admiralty method).
  • Optional UKHO ADMIRALTY predictions with your own subscription key.
  • Checked against NOAA official predictions: under 1 cm rms and under 2 minutes for HW/LW at three test ports.

Crow’s NestWind and weather
Weather

Wind from the model you trust, and the ocean picture

Wind

  • Seven forecast models: UK Met Office (default), ECMWF, DWD ICON, DMI HARMONIE, Météo-France, NOAA GFS, best match. Compare all at a chosen time.
  • Dial, Beaufort force, gusts and an hourly table; arrows point the way the wind blows.

Inshore waters forecast

  • The Met Office inshore forecast for the nearest of 19 areas plus Shetland, with warnings; the last good copy is kept and marked if the source is unreachable.

Atlantic weather chart

  • Ocean-crossing zones with wind, gusts, sea, swell, rain and isobars over 10 days, a fleet overview table and the boat’s position.

Crow’s NestPassage pack
Before sailing

A printed passage pack in two taps

Choose the dates, up to four ports and what to include; Crow’s Nest lays out tides, wind and the inshore forecast on A4 to print or save as a PDF for the chart table.

  • Tides: curve, HW/LW and hourly heights per day for each port.
  • Wind: chart plus 3-hourly or hourly tables, at your position or a chosen port.
  • Inshore forecast for the nearest or a chosen area.
  • Settings live in the link, so a saved pack opens the same way again.
  • Every export is branded: letterhead, copyright line and third-party credits.

Crow’s NestPhone app, security and status

The phone app

  • The same pages, bundled into iPhone and Android apps (Capacitor 8): they open with no signal and show the last known state.
  • Records made offline are saved on the phone first and sent in order when back in range; any that no longer fit are listed, never lost silently.
  • Older app builds keep working: the phone API only grows.

Security and data

  • Sign-in on every page and route; phones use their own device tokens, removable at once.
  • Crew accounts and phones are confined to their own records.
  • Strict browser security headers, formula-safe CSV, audited changes, automatic backups on each update.
